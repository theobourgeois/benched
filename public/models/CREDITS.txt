BENCHED — Model credits

skater.fbx
Mixamo character "Ch01" (non-PBR), T-pose, Mixamo humanoid rig (mixamorig12).
https://www.mixamo.com/
Characters and animations may be used royalty-free in games.
Any other Mixamo-rigged FBX with mixamorig bones can replace this file.

helmet-player.glb
Hockey helmet (Washington Capitals livery baked in). Sketchfab download.
Parented to the Mixamo head bone; the jersey crest is a separate team-logo decal.

helmet-goalie.glb
Goalie mask / cage helmet. Sketchfab download.
Parented to the Mixamo head bone on goalies only.

The Sketchfab "hockey mammoth jersey" scan was not used: it is an unskinned
display mesh (~388k tris, no UVs) and cannot follow a skating skeleton.

At runtime the skeleton is cloned per skater, the shirt/pants are team-tinted,
and the pose is applied in code (src/scene/skaterModel.ts, src/scene/skaterPose.ts).
Stick IK and knockdown ragdoll sit on top of the Mixamo bind pose.

hockey-stick.glb
"Hockey Stick" by grimren13, Sketchfab:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hockey-stick-9256b0ad52504ea38f013a76618428ad
License: CC BY-NC 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): attribution
required, non-commercial use only. Skater sticks only; goalies keep the procedural paddle stick.
At load the mesh is mirrored so the curve faces the forehand, the blade is lengthened and the
shaft section widened (src/scene/stick.ts, stickModel).
